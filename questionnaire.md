# Logbuch Questionnaire (Fragebogen)

Rough numbers beat none. When done, tell Claude: **`/intake`**. It will update `CLAUDE.md`, the life map,
OKRs, habits and the dashboard config from your answers and show you the diff for approval.
Items marked ★ are the ones that most change the plan; start there if short on time.
